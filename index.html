about

Curious about why models get things wrong.

I'm in the final year of a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, specialising in Data Science, at Presidency University in Bengaluru.

Most of my work sits where machine learning meets real decisions: catching fraud when it's one transaction in hundreds, spotting attacks in network traffic, and checking whether the features a model leans on are the ones it should.

I like results I can defend. That means held-out tests, honest baselines and saying so when an idea doesn't help.

I'm also the co-founder of hundrd, a Bengaluru digital studio that builds websites, dashboards, forecasts and machine learning tools for growing businesses. I lead the AI and data side.

Outside research I build practical things. I design workflow automations in n8n and have built five of them, each on a different architecture, using only free services. I also make bots and small web tools.

For Smart India Hackathon 2025 my team designed a multilingual, retrieval-based assistant that answers farmers' questions in regional languages. Right now I'm going deeper into NLP and large language models.
